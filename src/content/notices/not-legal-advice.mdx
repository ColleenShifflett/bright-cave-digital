This section describes how privacy and data protection regulation affects
what analytics data you can collect, retain, and analyze. It is written for
practitioners who need to understand the shape of the constraint.

It is not legal advice. Nothing here is a substitute for advice from
qualified counsel familiar with your jurisdiction, your industry, your
data, and your specific circumstances. Reading it does not create an
attorney-client relationship with the author or with Brightcave Digital.

Regulation in this area changes frequently, and enforcement interpretation
changes faster than the statutes do. Sections carrying a verification date
were checked against primary sources on that date and may have been
overtaken since.

Where this guide describes what a regulation requires, treat the
description as a starting point for a conversation with counsel rather than
as a conclusion you can act on.
